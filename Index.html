Executive Proposal Synthesis

DeepPlume-6000: Secure Benthic Telemetry Network

Fusing 6,000-meter autonomous sediment plume profiling with cryptographically authenticated GOES DCS satellite telemetry.

Strategic & Regulatory Imperative: On April 24, 2025, Executive Order 14285, "Unleashing America's Offshore Critical Minerals and Resources," directed federal agencies, led by NOAA, to accelerate seabed resource assessment while establishing rigorous environmental safeguards. Commercial extraction governed by the Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources Act (DSHMRA) of 1980 requires continuous monitoring of benthic sediment plumes utilizing the "best available technology".

Simultaneously, an exhaustive audit of NOAA's SBIR program repository (143 historical Phase I awards from FY18–FY26) reveals two profound, un-competed technical vulnerabilities: (1) Zero funded awards addressing environmental monitoring technologies for offshore critical minerals, and (2) A complete absence of cybersecurity protocols for the GOES Data Collection System (DCS) as NOAA restores two-way commanding (DCPC) across 33,600 global platforms.

An exhaustive review of 143 Phase I abstracts reveals distinct programmatic shifts away from broad climate modeling toward applied ocean economy, uncrewed platforms, satellite continuity, and hard economic outputs.

Meanwhile, explicit priority domains such as 'Omics (6%), citizen science (6%), and cybersecurity for environmental telemetry (0 awards) remain severely under-filled. The FY26 selection pivot—where Topic 9.3 (Ocean Economy) captured 58% of awards following the administrative funding lapse—confirms that FY27 reviewers heavily favor proposals backed by clear commercialization drivers and national security imperatives.
